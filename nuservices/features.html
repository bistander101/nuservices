✨ Features

A full front office, running on autopilot

NuServices does everything you'd expect from a great receptionist, dispatcher, and follow-up assistant — without breaks, sick days, or a salary.

🌙 Answering

Every call answered in your company's voice

NuServices picks up in under two rings, greets callers with your business name, and holds a natural, friendly conversation. It knows your services, your service area, your hours, and your prices — because you teach it once during setup.

  • Answers 24/7/365 — nights, weekends, and holidays included
  • Natural, human-sounding voice callers actually enjoy talking to
  • Handles multiple calls at once — no busy signal, ever
  • Custom greeting, tone, and scripts to match your brand
  • Answers FAQs: pricing, service area, licensing, warranties

📅 Booking

Jobs land on your calendar, not in your voicemail

NuServices reads your real availability and offers callers actual open slots. When they pick one, the job is booked, confirmed by text, and synced to the tools you already run your business on.

  • Two-way sync with your live calendar — no double bookings
  • Automatic SMS confirmations and appointment reminders
  • Collects name, address, phone, and job details up front
  • Smart scheduling rules: buffer times, service windows, zones
  • Reschedules and cancellations handled automatically
