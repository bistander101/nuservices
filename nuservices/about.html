🏢 About Us

Built for the people who keep the lights on

National Utility Services exists for one reason: the businesses that fix, maintain, and manage America's homes and buildings shouldn't lose work because they were busy doing the work.

📖 Our Story

It started with a missed call

Every contractor knows the feeling. You're elbow-deep in a job, your phone buzzes in your pocket, and by the time you can answer, the caller is gone — along with a few hundred dollars of work. Multiply that by every week of the year, and missed calls quietly become one of the biggest costs in the trades.

We built NuServices to close that gap. Modern AI can finally hold a real conversation, understand what a caller needs, and take action — so we trained it to do the one job that matters most for service businesses: answer the phone, be helpful, and book the job.

Today NuServices answers calls around the clock for contractors and property managers across the country, turning after-hours rings into booked revenue.
