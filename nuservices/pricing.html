Pricing questions, answered

Is there really no contract?

Correct — every plan is month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your dashboard. We keep you by answering your calls well, not with fine print.

What happens if I go over my monthly call limit?

We never stop answering your phone. Overage calls are billed at a simple per-call rate ($1.25 on Starter, $0.95 on Pro), and we'll suggest an upgrade if that would save you money.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing number and simply forward calls to NuServices — always, after a few rings, or only after hours. You control exactly when the AI picks up.

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with full features. No credit card required to start — we'll help you set up in about 15 minutes and you can hear the AI answer your line the same day.

What if a caller asks something the AI can't answer?

The AI gracefully takes a detailed message, tells the caller you'll follow up, and notifies you immediately. You can also enable live transfer so complex calls ring through to you or your team.

Can it handle tenant maintenance requests?

Yes — on the Scale plan, NuServices runs full maintenance intake: it logs the unit, the issue, urgency, and access permissions, then routes it to the right vendor or escalates true emergencies instantly.
